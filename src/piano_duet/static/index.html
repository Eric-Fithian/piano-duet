How to read your levels

Levels 1–5 are practice stages within this app, not exam grades. They use up to 12 recent assessed attempts per skill, requiring at least three attempts and 85% weighted accuracy at that stage. A skill’s overall level summarizes the material you tried; expand it to see which notes, keys, and conditions it covers. It does not establish mastery of untested material.

Pitch and rhythm are compared with the task. A steady slower tempo preserves your rhythm score; controlled speed uses the suggested tempo. Loudness is an approximate within-phrase estimate from microphone audio. Free improvisation contributes experience, with no “correct notes” or creativity score. Hearing or revealing an answer changes which skills the attempt can assess.

Recognized notes, prompts, help used, and assessment details are stored locally. Audio recordings are never saved. If the microphone gets something wrong, exclude the attempt and your profile will be recalculated.

Need a hint?

Middle C is just to the left of the pair of black keys nearest the center of your piano. The white keys going upward are C, D, E, F, G, A, B, then C again. The number after a note is its octave: middle C is C4. Take your time, then press Finish attempt or space. Using a hint is saved with your attempt.
