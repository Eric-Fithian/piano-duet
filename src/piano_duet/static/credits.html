MODELS & CREDITS

A few good collaborators.

Aria by Louis Bradshaw and collaborators, supported by EleutherAI. The 2025 piano transformer uses its generation-tuned checkpoint, quantized locally to 4-bit with MLX. Code and model: Apache 2.0.

High-resolution Piano Transcription by Qiuqiang Kong and collaborators. The microphone transcription model uses stelee’s ONNX conversion and the original regression decoder. MIT license.

Piano samples from Nicholaus P. Brosowsky’s tonejs-instruments, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Samples are pitch-shifted and enveloped for local playback.

Sheet music rendered with VexFlow 5.0.0, MIT license. The notation engine and its fonts run locally. Package license.

Interaction inspired by Google Creative Lab’s A.I. Duet, created by Yotam Mann with the Magenta and Creative Lab teams. This is an independent local application.

Audio is processed in memory on your Mac. It is not saved or uploaded. Save MIDI explicitly to keep the recognized notes and generated replies. The app makes no inference requests to outside services.
