# Folio: sign-off direction

The selected direction is the **topline** at every size. Portrait uses a compact top line and a bottom transport; short landscape places the same transport in the top line so the complete four-bar phrase can fit on one line. The microphone state and level sit between Listen and Start/Stop, with at least 12 px between controls. The score is the page, without a card or decorative phone gutters. Score options and zoom expand inside the chrome, never on top of notation. The result occupies the transport area below the score and keeps the note marks visible.

The stage path shows five noteheads as a window onto the real **24** Reading stages. Completed nodes use an ink check and gold fill; the current node has a heavier outline. A gold-filled ring shows progress to the next reader level. The beamed streak grows to four noteheads, with the exact number of first-try attacks in the label; it is absent at zero and hidden while playing. Drills shows its own drill level and best tempo; Library shows neither. The [UX principles](ux-principles.md) trace the real evidence, XP, level and streak rules and reconcile the illustrative result with them.

## Identity and type

The [two-voice master](master/two-voice.svg) is the sole mark: two tilted noteheads a third apart, the player's descending stem suggesting **p** and the listener's ascending stem suggesting **d**. The stems are equal 28 × 7 units, heads have 12 × 8.5 radii and tilt −20°; their joins avoid spurs. The artwork is translated 3 units upward for optical centring. Apple touch and App Store icons use a 0.8 artwork scale. A separate [pixel-hinted 16 px master](master/two-voice-16.svg) has whole-pixel 2 px stems and approximately 6 × 4 px heads. Dark uses `#9dd5b0`; the monochrome master uses `currentColor`. Maskable artwork is scaled to 0.69, inside the 36%-radius safe circle.

The horizontal lockup embeds that same master shape. Fraunces is fixed at weight 600, SOFT 0, WONK 0, optical size 72 for display and 48 in the header, then converted to paths. “Piano” and “Duet” share a baseline, separated by 0.25 em; the latter uses the mark colour. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next is the sole interface family, with lining tabular numerals. Locally served Latin-subset WOFF2 files and OFL licences are in `fonts/`.

The requested SVGs, favicons, PWA/Apple/iOS icons and share images are in [assets/](assets/). `node design/brand/generate-assets.mjs` regenerates them from the mark and outlined wordmark masters; `node design/brand/generate-wordmark-masters.mjs` regenerates the latter from the licensed font subset. `node design/brand/verify-assets.mjs` checks source geometry, shared lockup shape, outlined text, ICO sizes, raster dimensions/alpha and maskable safe-area bounds. No generated asset has been installed into production app or site files during this review phase.

## Colour and notation

[tokens.json](tokens.json) is the token source. Light reward gold `#e9b949` is a **fill** with ink text or a check, not brown text; dark uses `#efc15d`. Gold is reserved for earned moments and the filled part of the level ring. The score ink on warm paper is AAA. Night page is `#1c2a23` with `#f5ebd8` ink and heavier staff lines; the blue cursor edge `#65b6e8` is 6.70:1 against that page. The cursor is a translucent column behind the current note, with an opaque high-contrast edge; the ink, clef, time signature and fingering remain above or outside it.

The [contrast audit](contrast.md) covers text, boundaries, badges, the cursor and the error fill. The wrong-note fill is a deliberate reviewer-approved exception to AAA: it is a non-text graphic requiring at least 3:1 against both ink and paper. Its AAA-contrast ink ring, × and text provide the additional cues. Correct green is only about 2:1 against the ordinary score ink; each correct note therefore carries an ink ✓, including on Night page. Colour alone never communicates correctness.

## Implementation reference

The frames use the actual app, Library, Models UI and ScoreView/VexFlow renderer. To make the result numerically truthful and reproducible, the score shown is an injected regular stage-1 C/D/E assessment fixture, not the app's ungraded C4 introduction. It has 16 attacks and a single corrected note; the example's previous history, progress, audio level, note marks and result are static presentation states. It does not save attempts or implement a new scoring, microphone or appearance feature. The Library row is imported through the app from synthetic MusicXML.

The original app preloads a distinct next exercise; its score is reduced here to a one-line ready label. The prototype also injects a balanced two-bar portrait cap and tighter engraving spacing to demonstrate the chosen layout. Validate dense music, large type, playback, actual score editing and keyboard/touch flows before production; these captures are not a substitute for that work. The actual five-finger drill's final whole note is the ninth note held to the end of bar 3, not an empty system; the picker and Help keep the full title and explanation accessible.

The [base](reference/base.css) and [topline](reference/topline.css) styles preserve the signed-off visual treatment as reference, with topline loaded after base. They are not a production implementation: the `--folio-*` custom properties correspond to keys in `tokens.json` and need to be supplied for the selected theme. The static prototype states described above need real app data and behavior. A release still needs an accessibility audit of the rest of the app.

From the project root, install the root and site JavaScript dependencies with `npm ci` and `npm ci --prefix site`, and have `uv` available. Run `node design/brand/generate-wordmark-masters.mjs`, `node design/brand/generate-assets.mjs`, `node design/brand/verify-assets.mjs`, and `node design/brand/contrast.mjs`. The wordmark generator uses `uv` to supply its pinned font tooling.
