title: Your first session
description: Set up the microphone, press Space, and play.

Start the app with `piano-duet`. It opens in your browser at **http://127.0.0.1:8765**.

## Set up the microphone

Put the Mac or an external microphone near the piano. A quiet room helps. The browser's built-in voice noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain control are turned off to preserve musical attacks.

When you first press **Space**, the browser asks for microphone access. If you denied it, check the browser's site permissions and **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone**. Choose the input device in **Settings** before turning the microphone on.

## Press Space and play

**Space** is the one playing control. Press it, or click the **Space** key at the bottom, to start listening.

- **Reading** starts listening immediately. Space pauses and resumes.
- **Drills** end each take by themselves shortly after you stop.
- **Practice, Duet and Variations** listen for a one-second room calibration first, so keep quiet, then play. Press **Space** to finish your attempt. Duet and Variations can also answer after a pause.

The app fits one screen, with the microphone controls always at the bottom. Exercise details, help, settings and progress open in panels. **Mic off** releases the microphone and stops playback. **Stop** is also available during processing or playback.

## Pick a mode

| Mode | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Reading](/docs/modes/reading/) | Sight-reading fresh scores, one note at a time |
| [Drills](/docs/modes/drills/) | Scales, arpeggios, chords and Hanon at a steady tempo |
| [Library](/docs/modes/library/) | Learning full pieces from MIDI, MusicXML or a recording |
| [Practice](/docs/modes/practice/) | Guided exercises across technique, theory, ear and repertoire |
| [Duet and Variations](/docs/modes/duet/) | Improvising with Aria and imitating your own ideas |

Use **Try a demo** in Duet or Variations to run the whole audio → transcription → Aria → playback path without a microphone. Demonstrations never change your levels.

## If notes are missed or misheard

Microphone transcription is approximate. Room acoustics, pedal resonance, fast repeated notes and distant microphones can cause missing or extra notes. **Note confidence**, near the top of **Settings** under Listening, defaults to **0.80**. Raise it gradually if you get extra notes, or lower it if notes are missed. Every attempt can be reviewed and excluded under **Progress**, which recalculates your levels.
