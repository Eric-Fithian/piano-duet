## Everything you play, heard

There is no on-screen keyboard and no MIDI cable. Put your device near an acoustic or digital piano, tap **Start**, and play.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Reading" icon="open-book">
    Endless sight-reading that never repeats. Each four-bar score is composed for you by the **Aria** piano model, under the rules of a 24-stage curriculum, and aimed at the notes you miss most. Correct notes turn green as you play them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Drills" icon="rocket">
    Scales, arpeggios, chords and Hanon, with standard fingering. A take ends by itself when you stop, and a compact chart shows where your tempo drifted, which notes were late, and what was uneven.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Library" icon="document">
    Import MIDI, MusicXML or a piano recording, or record one yourself. The cursor waits for each note, and every section is coloured by how well you've been playing it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<div class="rainbow" />

## Feedback you can read at a glance

<Image class="screenshot" src={results} alt="A completed five-finger drill with its score and feedback on notes, timing and loudness." />

Drills judge you against **your own** tempo, not a forced one. Three clean takes in a row earn a steady tempo, and ten levels per drill track how many keys you've mastered and how fast. The meters and timeline show exactly where a take went wrong.

<div class="rainbow" />

## How it works

<ol class="pipeline">
  <li><strong>Listen</strong>Your browser captures microphone audio and processes it on your device.</li>
  <li><strong>Transcribe</strong>Piano transcription models turn audio into notes, timing and loudness.</li>
  <li><strong>Assess</strong>Each attempt updates your local reading and drill progress.</li>
  <li><strong>Compose</strong>Aria, a 650M-parameter piano transformer, writes music under constraints on your device.</li>
  <li><strong>Play back</strong>Sampled piano sound and engraved notation, both rendered locally.</li>
</ol>
